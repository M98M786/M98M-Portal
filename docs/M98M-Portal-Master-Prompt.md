### 8.0b UNIVERSAL APPROVAL GATE (Hasib's rule — applies to EVERY task, every department)
**No task completes itself. On every task submission, the employee waits for approval.**
- Employee flow: Pending → Working → **Submit** (with submission note / Item ID / evidence) → status `Submitted — awaiting approval`. The employee's part is done; the clock on their time-taken stops here.
- Approver flow: the approver is the task's assigner by default, or the pipeline's designated approver where one exists (hunts → Zaid; keywords/working → Zain or Management; listing quality → Hamza; Potential-CPC → Management). They see a **Pending approvals** queue and choose: **Approve** → `Completed` (counts toward targets, performance, celebrations) or **Return** → back to `Working` with a mandatory comment; the employee is notified with the comment attached.
- Nothing enters performance metrics, target counters, or shoutout triggers until APPROVED. Management additionally sees **approval lag** (submitted → decided time) per approver, so approvals themselves can't become the bottleneck silently.
- Notifications: submission → approver · approve/return → employee · anything sitting in `Submitted` past 12 working hours → escalates to Management.
