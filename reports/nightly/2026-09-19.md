| Fix | Where | Commit / deploy |

**The VAT gap.** `VAT_TO_HMRC` was only ever checked by `truthTier1`, on the single night a day was
"yesterday". If an account's day tab had not reached `sheet_rows` by 03:00 PKT, the check recorded
STALE — and nothing ever came back for it, because `truthTier3` re-reads exactly those rows for the
last six closed days but only re-checked `ACTUAL_PROFIT`. That asymmetry is the whole story behind
the board showing 57 stale VAT scopes against 18 stale profit ones. Amna Baji 15 Sept is the clean
example: 30 rows sitting in `sheet_rows`, profit PASS, VAT still reporting "0 row(s)". For a
VAT-registered business that is 57 days of VAT nobody had verified.

Tier 3 now emits `VAT_TO_HMRC` from the same loop, honouring the tab's own totals row the same way
profit does.

**Verified live** on the Truth Check page after deploy:

env var separately as `env_force` instead of letting it speak for the switch. Verified live:
