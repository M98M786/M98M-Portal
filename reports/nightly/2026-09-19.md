### 2. Seven tracking numbers eBay rejected, invisible to every sweep since 25 August — MADE VISIBLE

`trackings` holds 7 rows with `push_status` `FAIL:400` (5) and `FAIL:500` (2), from 25 Aug to
11 Sept. We hold a tracking number for each; the buyer's eBay order shows none.

Nothing was ever going to retry them. `trackingBackfill` selects orders
`WHERE NOT EXISTS (… t.tracking != '')` — the failed row **keeps its tracking number**, so the
filter reads it as "already has tracking" and skips it permanently. No other sweep looks at
`push_status` at all. A failed push is not retried, not alerted, not counted anywhere.

**Fixed the visibility, not the pushes.** Tier 1 now emits `TRACKING_PUSH_FAILED`, confirmed live
on the board:

> 7 tracking push(es) rejected by eBay and never retried, oldest 2026-08-25 14:26:16 — the order
> shows no tracking to the buyer

Deliberately `INFO`, not `FAIL`: the number is not wrong, the push failed. `FAIL` would fire the
A16 alert letter every 15 minutes for a condition nobody can clear automatically.

**Not re-pushed on purpose.** Writing tracking onto a buyer's eBay order is an outward-facing act
that can trigger eBay's own buyer notification — on orders 8 to 25 days old, with auto-messages
under an owner-ordered halt. That is Hasib's call. The seven order IDs are listed under Numbers.

trigger**, and AS triggers do not write engine `sync_state` at all. Its failures go to

`asRunJob` is `auth: 'sync'` and the key never enters a browser, and `activityTail` is a *runnable*,
not an `api()` action (calling it returns "unknown action").

Indirect check, inconclusive: no `listing_revision` tasks were created on 18 Sept. That is not
proof of failure — the sweep suppresses a repeat while an open task exists on the same item, and
79 `listing_revision` tasks are already open.

**Route for tomorrow:** read `ACTIVITY_LOG` in the Portal DB via the sheet htmlview (the trick used
on 18 Sept for the signals freeze) and grep `ERROR:zeroSales` around 07:00 PKT. Reported as
unverified rather than green.
