### 5. Ads alert refs carry the literal string "undefined" — MEDIUM

**113 bells across 16 refs since 27 September** look like this:

```
18x  adtool:alertm:A16:undefined:2026-09-27
12x  adtool:alertm:A16:undefined:2026-10-16
 8x  adtool:alert:A16:undefined:2026-10-16
```

`put(rule, scope, payload, meta)` stores `meta` on the finding, and the bell ref is built from
`(f.item_id || f.account || f.campaign_id)`. The A16 truth-check rule passes **no `meta`** — its
subject lives in its scope key (`metric_id|scope_key`) — so all three are undefined and the ref
takes the word.

This cuts both ways, and the masking direction is the one that matters: every A16 failure on a
given day shares **one** ref, so when two different registered numbers break on the same day, the
second one's bell can be deduped into the first and **never rung at all**. A16 is the rule that
says a number on the portal disagrees with its own source — exactly the alert that must not be lost.
The DB insert is already guarded (`f.account || ''`), so only the bell is affected.

Fixed in source, syntax-checked, patch at `reports/nightly/patches/2026-10-18-adtool-alert-ref.patch`:
the finding keeps its own `scope`, and a new `adtAlertRefKey(f)` falls back to it and can never
emit `undefined`. **Not deployed** — finding 2.
