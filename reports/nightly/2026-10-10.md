### 6. The day-book bell cried wolf for 21 consecutive nights — FIXED (awaiting deploy)

`engine:bookmissing:*` fired every single night from 19 Sept to 9 Oct — PKT 00:02, all six
accounts, three recipients — and on 20 of those 21 nights the book was written later that day.

the PKT day flips, yesterday's sales-analysis tab has not been written yet *because that tab is
written during the day that follows it*. The bell could not help firing, and tonight — when a book
really is late — it reads exactly like the twenty before it.

`selfTest`'s own "books match orders" check still covers day 1 — that is finding 1 above). Only the
**bell** now waits for a day to be a full extra day overdue, says `STILL not written`, and files
under its own ref (`engine:bookoverdue:`) so it does not collide with the day-1 family. One pass per
PKT day, cursor-stamped whether or not it rings.

On the data I have, the first thing it will say once deployed is the true one: *AZHAR ABRT, 8
October*.

## Not reachable this run — and why it is finally fixable

The page spot-check (`#tasks`, `#orders`, `#signals`, `#dispatchToday`), `deptPending`,
`pendingApprovals`, `autoMsgQueue`, the r72/r20 ladder desks and `createTask` are all session-gated
and were **not** checked. The staff figures above are reconstructed from the `tasks` / `users` /
`alert_log` / `orders` / `notif_live` dumps instead.

But the cause is now known, and it is **not** the password:

- The browser pane **is signed in to Google as m98m786@gmail.com** (script.google.com opens straight
  into My Projects).
- The portal's button even renders personalised — *"Sign in as Hasib · m98m786@gmail.com"*.
- Clicking it does nothing. No popup tab opens, no network request leaves the page, no console
  error. The GSI button lives in a cross-origin iframe and completes through a popup, and the pane
  will not open one.

So one signed-in portal tab left open — or a sign-in flow that does not need a popup — restores
half this checklist for every future night. That is watchlist 2.

Two mechanics worth keeping, both learned tonight:

- **The engine key route answers Cloudflare `error 1010` to plain `curl`/`urllib`.** It is not the
  key and not the IP — it is the User-Agent. With a normal browser UA the same request works, which
  is what made this whole run possible from the Mac instead of through the pane.
- **Never read a `backupDump` tail.** Same lesson as 8 Oct, applied this time: every count here
  comes from the complete paged dump, filtered locally.
