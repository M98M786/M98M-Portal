## Findings

### 1. 80 loss reviews could not be closed — and rang 35,014 bells doing it — HIGH · FIXED

`Tasks.gs` closes a `loss_review` on an exact string match: the submission note must be one of
`Changed advertising` · `Changed price` · `Decision by management — keep same`, verbatim, em dash
included. Anything else throws. My tasks only ever rendered the same free-text box every other task
type uses — `tkSubmitForm` had a special block for `listing_revision` and nothing for `loss_review`
— so the only way to close one was to retype one of those three strings character-perfect.

Nobody ever has. **All 80 loss reviews raised since the type shipped are open; 0 have ever reached
Completed; 10 sit in Working** where Zain opened them and could not send them. And `Signals.gs`
re-rings each one: 35,014 `loss:` bells in 30 days — 3,000 to 6,300 a day — to Husnain (12,812),
Yousaf (11,651) and Zain (10,485), with single items ringing 312 times in three days.

That one stream is 55% of all portal notifications. The bell is unreadable because of it: **64,210
of 64,215 notifications in the last 30 days are unread.**

**Fixed and live** (`fa0303f`, build `20260924-0314`). The submit form now renders those three as a
required pick for `loss_review`, and the send path posts the chosen string verbatim — no "Changed:"
prefix, no appended prose, nothing the server can refuse. Verified against live: the three values
the desk will send are byte-identical to `LOSS_RESOLUTIONS`, and HTML-escaping does not alter them.

Zain can clear the backlog today. Each close silences that item's alert permanently.

### 2. The nightly validation has cried wolf every night since 1 September — MEDIUM · fix committed, NOT deployed

`selfTestRun` check 6 asks whether the last two full days have rows in `ads_daily`. eBay's official
daily ad report for a day is only *asked for* by `adsReportKick` in the same 02:10 slot and takes its
own time to build, so yesterday's rows are legitimately absent when the battery runs minutes later.
Result: a red **"Validation failed — ad books present for \<yesterday\>"** letter to Management on
**24 consecutive nights, 1–24 September, no gaps**, for a report that was never actually missing.
An alarm that is wrong every night cannot warn about anything.

The fix gives yesterday a second witness — `adsIntradayFinal`, the closing spend the intraday
pipeline records for each dying day at rollover (tonight: `2026-09-23:463.71`). Books rows **or** a
real closing total both prove the ads kept running; only when both are empty did the ads go dark,
and that still fails. The day-before-yesterday keeps the strict test, so a report that genuinely
never lands is caught one night later rather than never.

Committed and pushed as `008059b`, syntax-clean, `engine-worker.txt` published — **but not
deployed** (Finding 8).
