📌 Golden Rules — Har Case Se Pehle
Golden rules
  1. THE ONE RULE: har claim evidence se match kare. AliExpress photos aur video par decide karta hai, wording par nahi. Jo photo/video mein nazar nahi aata, woh message mein nahi likha jayega — ek unsupported claim poora case reject karwata hai. Hard hona = invented hona NAHI.
  2. Tone hard, facts first: order, date, fault, test, evidence — phir flat refund demand. No pleading, no "small business", no "respectfully request". Soft language AliExpress ko stall karne ki jagah deti hai; hard facts nahi dete.
  3. Escalation ladder: 1) Supplier direct (48h) — zyada tar yahin settle ho jate hain. 2) No reply / refusal → formal appeal open karo. 3) Chat replies se push karo. 4) Supplier stall kare → dispute AliExpress ko escalate.
  4. Dispute kabhi promise par close nahi. Refund account mein show ho, TAB close — closing usually Buyer Protection khatam kar deta hai. Koi exception nahi.
  5. Deadlines: supplier stage 48 hours; stall ya fault admit hote hi 24 hours. Buyer Protection window se pehle dispute kholo — photos open case mein baad mein add ho sakti hain. Returned item ke pohanchne ka intezar kabhi nahi.
  6. Simple English, short sentences — suppliers ki English basic hoti hai. No idioms, no long paragraphs.
  7. Har message ke baad Saved Cases mein log karo: order no, supplier, dispute ID, status, deadline date. Deadline guzarte hi agla qadam — clock reset nahi hota.
Appeal ek hi dafa, mukammal
  • Appeal is tarah likhi jati hai ke reviewer wahin faisla kar de — chat stage tak jane ki zaroorat na pare. Har wo sawal jo woh puchta, appeal mein pehle se answered ho: testing, return, partial, replacement.
  • Numbered evidence list = har number ke against file waqai attached ho. Jo evidence list mein hai magar case par nahi, wohi appeal ko girata hai.
  • Evidence drip-feed kabhi nahi — sab kuch ek submission mein. Adhoori appeal ke baad "more evidence" ka round hi asal waqt zaya karta hai.
  • Reject ho jaye to naya claim mat jodo — A-16 Re-Appeal: wohi reason, wohi case, sirf missed point aur exact photo/frame batao.
  • Dispute box chhota ho to Condensed variant paste karo aur baaqi tafseel evidence ke sath upload — text kaatna nahi.
Evidence that wins
  • Item + packaging + shipping label EK frame mein — parcel ko order se link karta hai.
  • 10–20s video har "doesn't work / won't hold" case ke liye — video hi DOA aur feature cases decide karti hai.
  • Listing image vs item side by side — not-as-described ke liye.
  • Ruler in-frame — size/dimension claims ke liye; numbers readable hon.
  • Damage ka close-up — item par, sirf box par nahi.
Honesty gate — kab draft NAHI karna
  • Tracking "delivered" dikhaye aur "not received" chahiye — kabhi nahi. Yeh check hota hai aur account ki credibility jala deta hai.
  • Fault camera par dikhaya nahi ja sakta — pehle evidence banao, phir message.
  • Claim jo listing ne kabhi kiya hi nahi. In cases mein strongest honest route lo — usually not-as-described ya partial-refund push.
Partial / replacement offers
  • Item usable + issue minor → partial accept (paisa jaldi wapas). Item genuinely dead/damaged → hold for full — partial dead unit cover nahi karta.
  • Replacement decline sirf genuine reason par (feature asli nahi / customer already refunded) — warna replacement aksar fastest hal hai.
